# Easter Egg

In the HiBy OS firmware, there's an easter egg on the "About" page in the settings.

## Accessing the Easter Egg Page
1. Enter the main settings menu
2. Scroll all the way to the bottom and enter the "About" page
3. Press the HiBy logo a bunch of times, until the screen changes
4. Now you should be in the developer easter egg page :)

> [!NOTE]
> If you don't have the "About" page, it can be enabled through a single-line modification in the firmware.
>
> In `/usr/resource/set_functions.json`, change `{"about":0}` into `{"about":1}`

> [!NOTE]
> In order to exit the page, just hold a tap on the screen for a second or so.
